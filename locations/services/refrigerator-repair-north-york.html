What to tell us when the fridge is not cooling

Describe whether the refrigerator compartment, freezer, or both are warm. Include when the problem began, any displayed error code, unusual noises, water leaks and the appliance model number. These observations help distinguish a cooling fault from a door, airflow or ice-maker problem before the technician examines the appliance.

Before a visit to your North York home

Keep a clear path to the refrigerator and note any condo access, parking or elevator arrangements when booking. Photograph the model label and error display. Avoid removing electrical covers or working on refrigerant lines; leave internal diagnosis to the technician.

Repair questions

Does a warm refrigerator always need a new compressor?

No. Temperature problems can also involve doors, airflow, fans or controls. The symptoms alone do not identify the failed part. A diagnosis should come before deciding which part to replace.

What information helps with a water or ice-maker problem?

Note whether water is leaking inside or outside the appliance, whether the dispenser still works and whether ice production stopped suddenly. Share the model number and a photograph of the affected area when arranging service.
