Separate drain, spin and leak symptoms

A washer that retains water needs a different diagnosis from one that drains but will not spin. Note whether water remains in the drum, whether the door unlocks and which part of the cycle stops. For a leak, describe where the water appears and when it starts. Record the exact error code rather than guessing which part has failed.

Prepare the laundry area for a visit

Keep access to the washer clear and identify the appliance model. For a stacked laundry unit, tell us about the installation and any tight access before booking. Include condo entry, parking or elevator arrangements for your North York address. Leave electrical covers and internal repairs to the technician.

Repair questions

Does a washer that will not spin need a new motor?

Not necessarily. Spin problems can involve the load, drainage, door lock or drive system. The cycle stage and error code help guide a diagnosis; they do not by themselves prove that a motor replacement is needed.

What should I include when reporting a leak?

Tell us whether the leak begins while filling, washing or draining, and whether water appears near a hose, the door or underneath the machine. Include the model number and a photograph if available.
